# OpenTable and news update — 24 September 2026

The main food guide now includes 17 additional Saarbrücken restaurants verified against their OpenTable pages. The requested “Quaks” is listed by OpenTable as **Quacks Restaurant**. Existing ESPLANADE and LUUC entries were kept and given direct OpenTable reservation links. The OpenTable filter shows the new additions; a link to OpenTable’s [live Saarbrücken directory](https://www.opentable.de/region/saarbrucken-restaurants) provides the changing full catalog.

OpenTable scores and review counts in the guide are dated snapshots and are labeled OpenTable rather than Google. Published dining hours were used where a page distinguishes restaurant and bar hours. Restaurant photos for 16 additions were sourced from their OpenTable listing images; Leidinger’s Lust uses its official breakfast image. The images are stored locally so the cards render reliably. If another remote photo fails, the site displays a local illustration. Their exact coordinates have not been verified; in map view they appear as Google Maps address links below the existing pins.
