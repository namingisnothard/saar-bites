# Nearby France Michelin additions — 2026-10-05

The original eight Saarland restaurants remain the Saarland set. The catalog also includes five selected French restaurants within the wider Saarbrücken outing area. All appear in the [MICHELIN France 2026 list](https://guide.michelin.com/fr/fr/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/la-liste-complete-des-etoiles-du-guide-michelin-france-2026): La Merise in the two-star section, the other four in the one-star section. This is a curated selection, not a complete list of French starred restaurants.

| Restaurant | Star source | Venue source for menu, booking, contact and service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auberge Saint-Walfrid, Sarreguemines | MICHELIN France 2026, Grand Est / Sarreguemines | [Official German menu](https://www.stwalfrid.fr/de/menus) and [official contact/booking page](https://www.stwalfrid.fr/de/contact). The Michelin distinction belongs to the Gourmet Restaurant, not the separate Brasserie du Casino. |
| Toya, Faulquemont | MICHELIN France 2026, Grand Est / Faulquemont | [Official menus](https://toya-restaurant.fr/portfolio/nos-menus/) and [official contact page](https://toya-restaurant.fr/accueil/contact/). Published menu prices are €80, €130 and €170; the contact page lists Sunday through Tuesday as closed. |
| L’Arnsbourg, Baerenthal | MICHELIN France 2026, Grand Est / Baerenthal | [Official menus](https://www.arnsbourg.com/fr/restaurant-gastronomique-moselle.html), [official booking and closure notice](https://www.arnsbourg.com/fr/), and [Relais & Châteaux listing](https://www.relaischateaux.com/fr/restaurant/l-arnsbourg/) for weekly service. The official text menu does not show a current price, so the page does not assert one. |
| Yozora, Metz | MICHELIN France 2026, Grand Est / Metz | [Official menu](https://restaurant-yozora.com/fr/restaurant-chic-metz.html) lists Ikebana €95, Yozora €135 and Tsuki €175. [Official site](https://restaurant-yozora.com/fr/) links to Zenchef booking and gives evening-only Wednesday–Sunday service. |
| La Merise, Laubach | MICHELIN France 2026, Grand Est / Laubach (two stars) | [Official menus](https://lamerise.alsace/les-menus/) list lunch €98/€128 on Thursday and Friday, and five-/seven-course menus €175/€225. The page also provides booking, contact and weekly service information. |

Menu prices and service times can change. The page links to the official venue sources for confirmation at booking.
