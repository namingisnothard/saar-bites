# Saarland Michelin catalog — reviewed 21 September 2026

## Coverage and distinctions

The catalog includes all eight Saarland restaurants in the [MICHELIN Guide Germany 2026 annual list](https://guide.michelin.com/de/de/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/alle-sternerestaurants---michelin-guide-deutschland-2026), published 23 June 2026: Victor’s Fine Dining (3); ESPLANADE, GästeHaus Klaus Erfort, ATAMA and LOUIS (2 each); Hämmerle’s, Kunz and midi (1 each). Total: 14 stars. Two restaurants are in Saarbrücken. [Saarland selection](https://guide.michelin.com/de/de/saarland/restaurants).

Michelin distinctions are independent of Google ratings and the homepage’s personal Maps favorites. No Google ratings, community reviews, or live table availability were added or inferred. This is a standalone catalog linked from the homepage; regional venues do not enter the existing Saarbrücken open-now radar.

## Conflicting or stale details handled

- ESPLANADE’s general webpage still mentions Racine and longer Signature menus. The dated 16 September PDF takes precedence for the concrete menu and prices. The official reservation page is linked instead of an embedded OpenTable URL carrying a 2017 date.
- Erfort’s website retains old prose claiming three stars; the 2026 Michelin list confirms **two**, which is what the catalog uses. Its promotional €136 menu is omitted because timing/course details were not clear.
- LOUIS’s English page still gives €235/175 for the main menu. The current German page has different seasonal dishes and €240/180; it is the linked source.
- Kunz’s HTML mentions 4–7 courses and old 2023/2025 dates. Its currently linked PDF gives 4/5/6-course Signature and the traditional menu. The catalog flags that PDF as undated. Its signed expiring CDN URL is not hardcoded; the official menu page provides the maintained link.
- Hämmerle’s Michelin-starred Chef’s Table is distinguished from its separate Landgenuss restaurant and older Barrique-era descriptions.
- midi’s booking-page body retains 18:30 and directories retain the prior chef/schedule. The dated **23 September 2026** announcement takes precedence; this future effective date is displayed explicitly as of the 21 September review.
- No booking was submitted, no availability promised, and no deposits were paid. Booking links lead to restaurant-controlled pages or their explicitly linked booking provider.

## Le Flâneur follow-up

- [Opening report](https://www.neueroeffnung.info/saarbruecken/le-flaneur-franzoesisches-bistro-restaurant): expected week beginning 21 September, exact day unconfirmed; St. Johanner Markt 7–9, former Sausalitos. Previews oysters, poultry, bœuf bourguignon, sea bass, flammkuchen and a vegan foie-gras dish from ATAMA. This is a reported concept preview, not a published final menu or evidence that ATAMA’s chef is Le Flâneur’s chef.
- [Restaurant OpenTable listing](https://www.opentable.de/r/le-flaneur-saarbrucken): “Coming Soon” and explicitly no menu available when reviewed. The generic price category was not treated as verified dish pricing.
- [Operator’s cook vacancy](https://place2b.heyrecruit.de/?id=142323&location=36023&page=job) corroborates Place2b as the operator but does not identify the head chef.
- No named chef or full priced menu could be verified in the accessible sources. Similarly named restaurants in Paris and elsewhere were excluded. Le Flâneur remains in opening news, outside the starred catalog.
